🎯 Domain Selection Criteria
Criterion 1
Large Market
We select the segment with the largest volume of money — more room for growth and higher deal sizes
Criterion 2
Long Contracts
Contract length = season length. The longer the season, the more predictable the P&L
Criterion 3
Margin ≥ 50%
Unit economics must sustain profitability of at least 50% — otherwise scaling makes no sense
Criterion 4 · Startup Factor
Low Entry Barrier
We are a startup without brand recognition or reputation. We need a segment where decisions are made quickly and do not require years of relationship-building
